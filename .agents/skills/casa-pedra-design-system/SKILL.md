# Casa Pedra Design System

## 1. Architectural Geometry
- **Strict Square Borders:** All buttons, cards, tags, form inputs, and badges MUST use `border-radius: 0`. No rounded pills or circular buttons allowed.
- **Monolithic Blocks:** Containers should look carved from stone with solid borders (e.g., `1px solid rgba(2, 87, 95, 0.28)`).

## 2. Mineral Color Palette
- **Deep Teal (`--teal`):** `#02575f` (primary accent & dark sections).
- **Mineral Sand (`--paper` / `--bone`):** `#faf9f6` & `#f2efe9` (light natural surfaces).
- **Terracotta Amber (`--orange`):** `#ec8d00` (tactile focal points & active accents).
- **Deep Ink (`--ink`):** `#182a23` (high-contrast typography).

## 3. Commercial & Technical Clarity
- Explicit timelines (30–50 days for custom bespoke pieces).
- Factory anti-stain oleo-hydrophobic sealing guarantees.
- São Paulo apartment logistics, floor lifting, and showroom availability.
